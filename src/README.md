# SwapMart

Switzerland's fairest marketplace for trading, swapping and selling items. A modern alternative to Ricardo.ch with significantly lower fees and a unique barter/swap system.

**Live site:** [stuzzicadenti-ag.github.io/swapmart](https://stuzzicadenti-ag.github.io/swapmart/)

## What is SwapMart?

SwapMart is a Swiss-focused online marketplace that combines traditional buying/selling with a built-in bartering system. Users can sell items for CHF, swap items directly (item for item), or use "Swap + Cash" deals where they offer an item plus money.

## Key Differentiators vs Ricardo.ch

| Feature | SwapMart | Ricardo.ch |
|---------|----------|------------|
| Selling commission | **3%** | 8-12% |
| Free listings/month | **5** | Varies |
| Barter/Swap | **Yes** | No |
| Swap + Cash deals | **Yes** | No |
| Money Guard | **Free** | Mandatory (paid) |
| Max commission cap | **CHF 50** | CHF 290 |

## Features

- **Barter system** - trade items directly with zero fees
- **Swap + Cash** - offer item + money for flexible deals
- **3% commission** - significantly lower than competitors
- **5 free listings/month** on the free tier
- **Swiss Post integration** - tracked shipping with label generation
- **Local pickup** - built-in coordination for in-person trades
- **Verified profiles** - ID verification and public trade history
- **Secure messaging** - negotiate without sharing personal info

## Business Model

Three subscription tiers:

- **Free** - 5 listings/month, 3% commission, unlimited swaps
- **Seller Pro** (CHF 9.90/mo) - 30 listings/month, 2% commission, featured placement
- **Business** (CHF 29.90/mo) - unlimited listings, 1.5% commission, API access, analytics

Pure barter swaps (item for item) are always free across all tiers. Commission only applies to the cash portion of any transaction.
